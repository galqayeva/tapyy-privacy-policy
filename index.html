TAPY Privacy Policy

  

Last updated: November 4, 2026

  

    TAPY is a personal utility app that helps users save information     for quick copying and prepare money requests to share with others.     Your privacy is important to us.   

  

1. Information We Store

  

    TAPY allows you to create labels and associate information with them,     such as card details, addresses, and other text you choose to save.     This information is stored locally on your device, if that is how     the app is configured. Please do not store information you do not     want others with access to your device to see.   

  

2. How Your Information Is Used

  

    Information you save is used to provide the app's features, including     copying saved text to your clipboard and preparing money-request     messages when you choose to use that feature.   

  

3. Data Collection and Sharing

  

    TAPY does not operate its own server to collect or store the     information you save, provided the app is used without any     additional data-transmitting services.   

  

    When you choose to share a message through another app or service,     the information you share may be processed by that service according     to its own privacy policy. Only share information with recipients     you trust.   

  

4. Clipboard

  

    When you tap an item to copy its contents, the information is placed     on your device's clipboard. Other apps may be able to access     clipboard contents under the applicable operating system rules.     Avoid copying sensitive information when unnecessary.   

  

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  

    Saved information remains on your device until you delete it or     remove the app and its locally stored data. The exact deletion     behavior may depend on your device settings and backups.   

  

6. Data Security

  

    We recommend protecting your device with a passcode and keeping     its operating system up to date. No method of electronic storage     is completely secure.   

  

7. Children's Privacy

  

    TAPY is not designed to collect personal information from children.     If you believe personal information has been collected through     the app, please contact us.   

  

8. Changes to This Policy

  

    This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated     version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
